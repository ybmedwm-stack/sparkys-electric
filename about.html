Mark "Sparky" Donovan pulled his first permit in 1998 working under a master in South Portland. By 2004 he had his own master's license and a single Ford E-150 with the company name lettered by hand. Today the shop runs out of the same garage on Munjoy Hill — just with four trucks instead of one, and Mark's son Ben working alongside him.

HOW WE WORK.

You call, we answer. Mark or Ben picks up during business hours. After-hours, the same number rings the on-call electrician — no answering service, no offshore call center routing you somewhere you didn't expect.

Estimates are estimates, not opening bids. When we quote a price, that's the price. If we hit something genuinely unexpected during the job (old houses do that), we stop and call you before we spend your money.

Code-correct, every job. Inspections sign off the first time because the work was done right the first time. We don't cut corners we'd be ashamed of if our own inspector showed up unannounced.

Clean job site, every time. We bring drop cloths. We wear booties in the finished spaces. We sweep up. We label every breaker so the next person — whether it's us, you, or whoever owns the house in 20 years — knows exactly what's what.

Two-year workmanship warranty. Everything we install. If something we did isn't right, we come back and make it right. That's the deal.
