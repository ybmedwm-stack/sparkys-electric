What we do

Residential, commercial, and the weird stuff in between.

Whatever's behind your walls, we've probably already worked on it.

Panel upgrades

100A, 200A, 400A service. Federal Pacific replacements. Subpanel installs for additions and detached buildings.

EV chargers

Level 2 home charger installs — Tesla, ChargePoint, Wallbox, JuiceBox. Load calculations done right so you don't trip breakers.

Generators

Whole-house standby (Generac, Kohler, Cummins) and portable transfer switches. Maine winters don't ask permission.

Smart-home wiring

Lighting control, Cat6, security pre-wire, low-voltage. Done at framing or retrofitted into a finished house without trashing the walls.

Troubleshooting

The lights that flicker only on Tuesdays. The outlet that's hot to the touch. The breaker that pops "for no reason." We find it.

Code & inspection

Pre-purchase inspections, insurance claim documentation, code corrections, town/AHJ sign-offs. Paperwork done properly.
